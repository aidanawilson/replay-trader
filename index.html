Privacy & Anonymous Research

Replay Trader is designed to collect useful simulated-trading research without building a profile of who submitted it. Research sharing is enabled by default and can be turned off in Settings at any time.

Research records may include
  • Historical chart date/time, market, contract, timeframe, and trade direction
  • Entry, stop, target, simulated risk, P/L, R, duration, and outcome
  • Simulated account settings and higher-timeframe bias
  • Trade classifications, indicator settings, and trade-management actions
Research records are not designed to include
  • Name, email, username, social identity, or account login
  • A persistent contributor, browser, or device identifier
  • Real brokerage credentials, payment information, or real brokerage account data
  • Precise location, device fingerprint, or an IP address stored in the research database

How trade updates work

Each contributed trade receives a random identifier for that trade only. It is used so that later edits to the same trade—such as changing classifications—can update that trade's research record. The identifier is not intended to link separate trades to the same person or browser.

Your choice

Research sharing is on by default. Turning it off stops future research submissions while it remains disabled. Clearing your local Trade Log, resetting a simulated account, deleting a local trade, or clearing browser storage does not delete research records that were already contributed. Replay Trader does not provide a user-facing database deletion control.

Purpose & retention

Contributed records may be retained for long-term aggregate and statistical research into simulated trading strategies, setups, market conditions, and simulator performance. Replay Trader does not sell research records or use them for targeted advertising.

Local browser storage

Replay Trader uses browser storage to remember simulator settings, preferences, account bookkeeping, and the local Trade Log. The site does not intentionally use advertising cookies, third-party marketing pixels, or cross-site tracking identifiers.

Cloudflare infrastructure

The website, research API, and database use Cloudflare infrastructure. Cloudflare may process standard technical network information—such as IP addresses, routing data, and request metadata—as necessary to deliver and protect its services. Replay Trader's research database is designed not to store an IP address with a trade. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy for Cloudflare's own practices.

Questions & changes

Privacy questions can be directed to the creator through the @_aidan_w link shown in Replay Trader. This notice will be updated if the research data model, service providers, or data practices materially change.

This is a private, browser-based replay simulator built for educational chart practice and simulated trade review. It is not a broker, signal service, trading system, financial adviser, investment adviser, tax adviser, legal adviser, prop-firm service, or professional advice platform.

Trade-log bias is calculated at entry using the last completed 4-hour Supertrend candle with ATR length 12 and multiplier 3.5. The simulator may display historical, imported, sample, or market-style data for practice. Open positions are automatically liquidated and pending orders canceled at 4:59 PM ET, with the 5:00–6:00 PM ET maintenance break marked on the chart. Data and simulated results are provided only for private educational testing. Do not copy, resell, republish, or redistribute any market data from this site.

Settings, preferences, account bookkeeping, and the local Trade Log may be saved in your browser. When anonymous research sharing is enabled, simulated trade records are also sent to the Replay Trader research database as described below.

How Replay Trader Works

Replay Trader drops you into historical market situations so you can practice analysis, execution, and decision-making without knowing what happens next.

Trade the chart normallyPan, zoom, change timeframe, choose a contract and quantity, place simulated entries, and use the left toolbar for drawings and indicators.
Manual practiceTake as long as you want to study each random scenario. Advance candles when needed, trade or skip, review the result, then move to the next scenario.
Drill modesSlow, Medium, and Fast add timed analysis and decision stages, then automatically advance after review for faster repetition.
Simulated accountYour trades affect a numbered practice evaluation with configurable size, drawdown model, commissions, and risk rules. Reach the profit target to pass it, or breach the limits to fail it; the next evaluation receives the next account number.
Trade log & setup tagsConfirmed attempts are logged automatically. Click a Recent Trade to tag its setup, then use Trade Log & Analytics to see what is and is not working.
Focus filters & sessionsUse Focus Filters to drill specific global sessions or AM/PM periods. The Sessions indicator can shade Tokyo, London, and New York directly on the chart.
Chart toolbarDrawing tools, EMA/DEMA, Supertrend, Sessions, sound, and Chart Settings live on the left toolbar. Appearance settings can be customized and reset.
Settings & market dataBuilt-in NQ1 and ES1 data stay available at all times. You can also upload a separate compatible 1-minute OHLC CSV and select it as User Uploaded.
Use Manual mode for deeper chart study or a Drill mode for fast repetition. Your preferences and practice data are saved locally in this browser.

Session

Timeframe is controlled from the chart toolbar. Scenarios open at a readable zoom, while additional pre-decision history is available for context: about 1 day on 1m, 2 days on 5m, 3 days on 15m, and 4 days on 1h/4h. Scenario selection never starts inside the maintenance break.

Trade-log bias is calculated at entry using the last completed 1-hour Supertrend candle with ATR length 12 and multiplier 3.5.
